Privacy Policy

Joburg Hospital Management System

This policy explains how we collect and use your information.

  1. Information we collect: name, email, phone number, ID number (patients), role, appointment details, clinical notes, prescriptions and billing-related data needed to provide care.
  2. How we use it: to create and manage your account, schedule visits, support clinical care, dispense medication, process invoices and improve hospital services.
  3. Who can access it: authorised staff only (for example administrators, doctors and pharmacists) according to their role.
  4. Storage and security: data is stored in the hospital system with access controls. You must also protect your password.
  5. Sharing: we do not sell your personal information. Data may be shared only where required for care, payment or legal obligations.
  6. Your choices: you may request updates to your profile details through the system. For further privacy requests, contact the hospital administration.
  7. Retention: records are kept as needed for healthcare, billing and legal requirements.

By registering, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.
